  • The iPhone app is designed to keep your information on your iPhone. The app's own code contains no network calls, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. What leaves your iPhone

Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you send it. The app has a share action for your summary (a document) and a test log. The test log is built to exclude audio and the detailed sound measurements. You choose where it goes in the iOS share sheet, and the app itself sends nothing. Once you share something, the receiving app or person handles it under their own rules.

The app can open links you tap, for example to a published source. Opening a link takes you to that website, which has its own privacy practices.

Backups

The app is designed to keep the saved-clips folder out of iPhone backups; this has not yet been confirmed on a device. Night summaries, answers and settings are not excluded from iPhone backups, so if you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, those files can be included in that backup under Apple's terms. They contain timings and sound-level summaries, not audio.

Apple's "privacy nutrition label"

Apple defines "collecting" data as transferring it off the device in a way that lets the developer or its partners access it for longer than needed to serve the request in real time. Because the app is designed to keep your information on your iPhone, our App Store privacy details report that we do not collect data. This is our reading of Apple's definition and we will update it if the app changes.

The web version

The web version runs in your browser. Your answers are kept in your browser's session storage and are cleared when you close the tab or choose "Delete my data". A recording you add is analyzed in your browser and is not stored by the page. The page's content security policy only allows it to connect to its own site. Sensitive information and who may be recorded

Sleep sounds and health answers are sensitive. Recording picks up everyone in the room, so please tell anyone who shares your room. The app asks you to confirm this before your first night.

Your choices

  • Delete one night, delete all saved clips, or delete everything with "Delete my data" in Settings.
  • Turn off the microphone, notifications or motion access in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Withdraw consent in Settings.

Children

SleepSmooth is for adults. It is not directed to children.

Medical information

SleepSmooth is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition. A quiet, normal-looking or inconclusive result cannot rule out sleep apnea. If you are concerned about your sleep or symptoms, talk to a qualified healthcare professional.

Changes

If we change what the app does with your information, we will update this page and the date above before the change ships.
