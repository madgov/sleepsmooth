SleepSmooth support

SleepSmooth is informational and is not a diagnosis. It cannot confirm or rule out sleep apnea. It is not for emergencies. If you feel unwell or are worried about your health, contact a qualified healthcare professional or your local emergency number.

Common questions

What does the app do?

It helps you notice and organize what you see about your sleep, listens during the sleep hours you set to summarize snore-like sounds, and helps you prepare a summary to discuss with a clinician. It does not measure breathing or airflow.

Recording did not run all night

Keep the app started and the phone plugged in if you can. A phone call, an alarm, another app using the microphone, or a low battery can interrupt recording; the app records what it knows about interruptions and shows them in the night's details. Recording with the screen locked has not yet been confirmed on every iPhone, so please treat a missing stretch as "not recorded", not as "quiet".

The microphone or notifications are off

Open iOS Settings, find SleepSmooth, and turn on Microphone or Notifications. The app still lets you look at the labelled sample night without them.

How do I delete my information?

In the app, open Settings. You can delete one night, delete all saved clips, or choose "Delete my data" to remove everything the app stores. Deleting the app also removes its files from the phone. On the web version, "Delete my data" in the page footer clears your answers and results from the browser tab.

Why does the result say it cannot rule out sleep apnea?

Because phone sound and a short questionnaire cannot show whether someone has sleep apnea. A quiet night, a low score or an inconclusive recording are not reassurance. Only a clinician, usually with a sleep test, can say.

The "sample night" is not mine

Sample nights are labelled "Sample" on purpose. They show how the app looks and are not your data.

Reporting a problem or a wrong sound label

You can mark a saved clip as a mistake inside the app. For anything else, email the address above and tell us the iPhone model, the iOS version and what you expected.
